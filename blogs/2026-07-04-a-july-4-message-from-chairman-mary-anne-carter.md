NEA Chairman Mary Anne Carter reflects on the agency's role in America's 250th anniversary celebrations, including grants supporting projects like New West Symphony's Ronald Reagan tribute and the NEA's Legacy Stage at the Great American State Fair. She highlights initiatives such as the NEA Big Read and Poetry Out Loud as part of the America250 commemoration.
