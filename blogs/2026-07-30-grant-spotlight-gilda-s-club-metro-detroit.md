Photo courtesy of Gilda's Club Metro Detroit A cancer diagnosis can be isolating, overwhelming, and difficult to discuss. At Gilda's Club Metro Detroit, the arts help people affected by cancer find another way to express what they're feeling—offering opportunities for connection and healing.
