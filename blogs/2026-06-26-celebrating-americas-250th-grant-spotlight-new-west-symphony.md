New West Symphony premiered "A Ronald Reagan Portrait," a multimedia symphonic work exploring President Reagan's life through music, photographs, and speeches, supported by an NEA America250 grant. The piece premiered at the Ronald Reagan Presidential Library, tracing Reagan's journey from Hollywood to the presidency.
