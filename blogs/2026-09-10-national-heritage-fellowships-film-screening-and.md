National Heritage Fellowships Film Screening and Conversations Body WHAT : The National Endowment for the Arts will premiere eight short documentary films about the 2026 National Heritage Fellows. Together, their stories celebrate the breadth of folk and traditional arts that exist in this country and help tell the story of America. The film screening will be followed by a conversation with the honorees.
