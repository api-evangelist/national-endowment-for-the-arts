National Heritage Fellowships Awards Ceremony Body WHAT : National Endowment for the Arts Chairman Mary Anne Carter and members of Congress will honor the 2026 National Heritage Fellows at an awards ceremony, which will include presentation of medals, remarks by the recipients, and short musical performances by several of the honorees. This event is hosted by the American Folklife Center of the Library of Congress. WHEN : Wednesday, September 30, 2026, beginning at 5:30 p.m.
